NEED SUPPORT?

If something online feels unsafe, you don't have to handle it alone.

Stop the conversation if possible, avoid sending additional information, save evidence when safe, block or report the account, and tell a trusted adult.

👨‍👩‍👧 Trusted Adult

Talk to a parent, guardian, teacher, counselor, or another adult you trust.

🚨 Immediate Danger

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services or seek help from a trusted adult.

📢 Report

Use the platform's reporting tools and appropriate official reporting channels.
