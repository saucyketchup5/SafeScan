What is Project SAFE SCAN?

SAFE SCAN is a youth-led initiative that aims to raise awareness about Online Sexual Abuse or Exploitation of Children (OSAEC) and Child Sexual Abuse or Exploitation Material (CSAEM).

The project promotes safer and more responsible online behavior while helping young people recognize online risks and understand where they can seek help.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower young people with knowledge, awareness, and safe digital habits.

👁️

Our Vision

A safer digital space where young people understand their rights and recognize online risks.
